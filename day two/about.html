About QuickNotes

QuickNotes helps you capture ideas the moment they arrive, without menus or friction. It is designed for students, writers, and anyone who thinks faster than they type.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Open the app and click New note.
  2. Type your idea — the note saves automatically.
  3. Press Ctrl + K to search any note instantly.
  4. Tag notes with #work or #idea to group them.

Features

  • Instant autosave
  • Keyboard-first navigation
  • Tag-based organisation
  • Dark mode
  • Export to Markdown

Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcut Action
Ctrl + N New note
Ctrl + K Search notes
Ctrl + S Force save
